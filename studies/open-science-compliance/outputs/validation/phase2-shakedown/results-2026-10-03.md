    the Amethyst→Disc cell (0.86717). The pilot's comparison table listed
    "Published 0.87" against Amethyst→Disc, which the text does not say.
    The values are identical in both attempts. Attempt-02 flags the
    paper's misattribution where the pilot re-mapped it.
- **Label:** pending your T02 and T06 rulings. If T02's adjustment is

  the adjustment changes what is computed, the pilot's SUCCESSFUL was too
