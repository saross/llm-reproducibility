# Opus 5.5 validation arms — results (2026-10-04)

**What this is.** The results of the three Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`)
benchmark arms that the design note (`design-note.md`) specifies. It covers
the gate statistics from two independent derivations, the measured
effort-to-cost profile, and the pre-declared selection rule (design note
Q4) applied to every eligible configuration.
**Status: computed by rule; awaiting the registrant's confirmation.** No
selection is final until Shawn confirms it.

## Effort, cost, and output (design note question 2)

Scoring spawns only; the Haiku reconciliation is about $1.2 per arm in
every Opus 5.5 arm. Prices are from the claude-api skill's table (cached
2026-09-25): Opus 5 $5 / $25; Opus 5.5 $4 / $20 per million tokens. Cache
reads are 0.1× input on Opus 5 and 0.05× on Opus 5.5; cache writes are
1.25× on both (all writes in these arms are 5-minute).

| Configuration | API requests | Output tokens (recorded → central) | Median spawn wall-clock | Cost: recorded / central / upper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| opus-5-5 medium | 49 | 114,502 → 142,175 | 88 s | **$8.70 / $9.25 / $9.37** |
| opus-5-5 high | 55 | 152,775 → 208,400 | 132 s | $9.55 / $10.66 / $10.80 |
| opus-5-5 xhigh | 69 | 479,726 → 512,614 | 305 s | $16.33 / $16.99 / $18.28 |
| opus-5 high | 97 | 249,033 → 374,261 | 286 s | $17.27 / $20.40 / $22.88 |
| opus-5 xhigh | 94 | 390,165 → 445,805 | 370 s | $19.46 / $20.85 / $22.48 |

- **The cost bounds exist because of register F-019.** Some requests'
  final usage is never written to the transcript, so their output is
  under-counted. `recorded` is the lower bound. `central` and `upper`
  impute the affected requests from the median or maximum of complete
  requests of the same kind (`selection-cost.py`, `selection-cost.json`).
- **Effort is the main cost lever on Opus 5.5.** xhigh writes 4.2× the
  output of medium recorded (3.6× at the central estimate), at no gain in
  concordance. Cache writes form a
  floor of about $6.1 per arm whatever the effort.
- **Opus 5.5 spends fewer requests per spawn and reads less cache** than
  Opus 5: 1.6–2.9M cache reads against 4.3–4.4M.
- **Served-effort signal (residual R1).** Output tokens and wall-clock both
  rise monotonically from medium through high to xhigh. That is consistent
  with the effort pin being honoured.
- **Against the API gate estimate:** the block cost about $38 recorded
  (about $41 at the central estimate) with reconciliation, against about
  $42 estimated.

## The pre-declared rule (design note Q4)

1. **Eligible configurations:** all three Opus 5.5 arms plus the two
   registered opus-5 arms. fable-5 is eligible but not selectable on price
   (amendment 1 §3).
2. **Cheapest eligible:** `claude-opus-5-5` at effort **medium** ($8.70
   recorded). The answer is robust to F-019, since medium's upper bound
   ($9.37) is below the next configuration's lower bound (high, $9.55).
   Agreement differences inside the confidence interval are not grounds for
   selection. xhigh's higher stability (145/150) therefore does not bear on
   the choice.
3. **Selection by rule: `claude-opus-5-5` at effort `medium`.** If the
   registrant confirms it, it enters amendment 3 (running-list item 5) and
   is lodged before census scoring. The registered regression gate and the
   pre-census supplement check then run on this configuration.

## Points for the registrant

- **F-019 (new, for ruling).** It bears on the 2026-10-04 D4 record as
  well. Opus 5 `high`'s "about 11% cheaper than xhigh" falls to about 2% at
  the central estimate and reverses at the upper bound. That is moot if
  Opus 5.5 is selected, but the ruling record should carry a note.
- **Error profile shift.** The registered opus-5 arms' errors are all
  over-credit (BI excluded: 11/0 and 14/0). Opus 5.5's are mixed (7/3 at
  medium). It is no longer a purely lenient scorer, which matters for how
  census bias is described.
- **A payload whose total disagrees with its items.** In medium run 3,
  `dye-et-al-2023`, `code_fair`, the recorded `total` is 7 but the
  sub-principle items sum to 8 (flagged by H13). The gates use items, so
  they are unaffected. But the schema does not enforce total = item sum.
  Census analysis should derive totals from items, or the census should add
  a check.
- **`data_fair available: false` on key-et-al-2024** in 5 of the 9 Opus 5.5
  runs (fable-5 had 1). The scores are used as recorded, as before.
- **R2 (relayed user message).** Arm 1's spawns received the neutral `Go`;
  arms 2 and 3, launched from task-notification turns, received nothing.
- **Handoff procedure correction.** The continuity's step 4 omitted
  `--contract-schema` and `--out <run_dir>/reconciliation-authoritative`,
  which the assembler requires. Every accepted pass here used the full
  form (arm 1's run notes).
