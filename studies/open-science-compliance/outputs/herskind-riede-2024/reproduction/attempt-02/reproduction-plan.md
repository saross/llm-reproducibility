## Exclusions

- Figure 1 (photographs of four decorated artefacts) (p. 2, Fig. 1): Photographs supplied by the National Museum of Denmark. No data analysis or computational output to reproduce.
- Supplementary Information S1 (Herskind&Riede_S1.xlsx) (Zenodo v2 deposit): Input data for the analysis, not a published result. Integrity is checked by md5 in materials, and S1 is used read-only for the verification aids.
- Supplementary Information S2 (Herskind&Riede_S2.R) (Zenodo v2 deposit): Analysis code: the means of reproduction, not a result.
- Analytical parameters: skip distance k = 13, t (482 in code), and frequency thresholds (> 9, > 4, > 2, observed >= 3) (p. 3, Section 2.2; Table 2 caption; S2.R): Inputs chosen by the authors, not computed results. k = 13 rests on the data-derived maximum of 15 motifs, which is enumerated as T03.
- Culture-complex date ranges (Maglemose ~11,000-8400 BP, Kongemose ~8400-7400 BP, Ertebølle ~7400-5950 BP) (p. 2, Section 2): Taken from the literature, not computed in this paper.
- Claim that the South Scandinavian material is 'nearly half of the total number of European Mesolithic ornamented artefacts listed by Płonka (2003)' (p. 2, Section 2): Qualitative comparison with an external catalogue total that the paper does not analyse and the deposit does not contain. Treated as a descriptive literature claim, not a named analytical value. Flagged for the approver, who may add it as an expected-untestable target before lock (R2).
- Illustrative ornamentation string 'A1 B10 C1 E1 F5 F7 F10 F12 F14 F49' (p. 3, Section 2.1): An example of the data format chosen to illustrate the method, not a result. Flagged for the approver.
- Identification of the five-motif Ertebølle cluster (C4, C5, C12, C13, D7) (p. 4, Section 3; Fig. 4 caption; Fig. 5): A qualitative identification shown in Fig. 4 and Fig. 5. It is scored within T11 (cluster box) and T14 (pie compositions) rather than as a separate named value, to avoid double counting under R3.
- PMI formula as printed (PMI = log2(observed/expected)) (p. 3, Section 2.2): A method statement, not a published value. Planning found that the code uses natural log(), and the deposited S3 value for I5 I13 (obs 3, token frequencies 6 and 4, t = 482) equals ln(3/0.04979) = 4.0985, which matches Fig. 3b. All published PMI values therefore appear to be natural-log values. The executor should record this text/code inconsistency as a finding. It does not change any target's scoring.

## Compute strategy

One deterministic run inside Docker on the study reference hardware. Estimated wall-clock: Docker build about 20-45 minutes (source compilation of quanteda and its dependencies), analysis under 5 minutes. Total about 1 hour against a 168-hour cap. A second run is optional and only confirms determinism (no random number generation is used). Parallelism is unnecessary at this scale. The wrapper (run-analysis.R) changes execution mechanics only. It sets the working directory, sources S2.R with UTF-8 encoding and echo, and captures the console output (table() and summary() results for Tables 1 and 2) to a log. It may run S2.R part by part (by the script's own part boundaries) so that intermediate objects (bigrams/trigrams/quadrigrams data frames, the per-panel skipgramsFreq/skipgramsPMI tables that later parts overwrite) can be saved to CSV between parts. No authors' statement is modified. A separate, clearly labelled comparison script holds all verification aids (ruling R1).
